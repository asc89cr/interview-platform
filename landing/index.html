FAQ

Questions, answered

Is this allowed? Is it ethical?

InterviewAce is built as a preparation and real-time support coach. You are responsible for using it appropriately and lawfully. Recording-consent laws vary by state and country — some require all parties to consent. Before any live session we ask you to acknowledge that you'll comply with the laws that apply to you. When in doubt, use it to practice.

Do you record the interviewer without their knowledge?

Audio is processed in real time to generate your talking points and is not stored by default. Transcript and audio retention are opt-in, controlled by you, and deletable at any time. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Which platforms does it work with?

Any video platform that plays through your computer's audio — including Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet. The desktop app captures your system audio locally.

What are the system requirements?

Any modern Windows laptop (macOS coming soon). No GPU required — all the heavy AI runs in the cloud.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Manage or cancel your subscription anytime from the billing portal. Free stays free.
