## Instructions
Use the STAR framework (Situation → Task → Action → Result) to structure the answer.

- **Situation**: Set the scene briefly (1–2 sentences).
- **Task**: State what was expected of you.
- **Action**: Describe 2–3 specific actions *you* took (use "I", not "we").
- **Result**: Share a measurable or qualitative outcome and what you learned.

Draw from the candidate's real resume and experience above when possible.
Keep the total answer under 200 words — brevity signals confidence.
Do NOT start with "I" — vary sentence openers for the Situation.
Do NOT include meta-commentary like "Great question".
